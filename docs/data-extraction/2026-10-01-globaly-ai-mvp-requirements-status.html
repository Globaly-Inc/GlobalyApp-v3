Overview

The Globaly AI MVP lets institutions onboard onto GlobalyApp through invitation-only registration, import or extract their institutional data, configure an AI Counsellor, and embed that counsellor into their own website.

Seven core areas:

  • Website & lead capture — §2
  • Institution invitation & onboarding — §3
  • Institution data extraction & management — appendix A (detailed requirements not yet supplied)
  • Business & contact management — appendix B (not yet supplied)
  • AI counsellor & knowledge management — appendix C (not yet supplied)
  • AI widget & conversation management — appendix D (not yet supplied)
  • Lead capture, feedback & AI learning — appendix E (not yet supplied)
Requirement text ends mid-§5.1 The supplied document stops at “A warning/confirmation mechanism should be presented d…”. §2–§5 below carry the requirements verbatim; the five areas with no requirement text are tracked in the appendix from code only, so the gap list stays complete. Send the rest and they move up into numbered sections.

Website & lead capture

Partial

2.1 Public website

Done

Visitors should be able to browse the Globaly website and access information about the product and services.

  • Separate Next.js app at globalyai-website/ — hero, product sections, demo film, cookie consent, and Terms / Privacy / Cookies pages.
  • Content is in code (src/lib/site.ts, src/components/v6/*); there is no CMS, so copy changes are deploys.

2.2 Lead conversion — book a meeting

Done

Visitors can book a meeting directly with Amit or the Sales Team.

  • Every CTA on the site is one component pointed at one URL — CAL_BOOKING_URL = https://cal.com/amit-ranjitkar/globaly-ai-intro-meeting (globalyai-website/src/lib/site.ts:7, used by CtaButton in components/v6/primitives.tsx:83).
  • Scheduling, reminders and the booking notification are Cal.com's, not ours.

2.2 Lead conversion — submit information

Not built

Visitors can submit their information through the website.

  • No form exists anywhere in globalyai-website/src — the only <form> and mailto: hits are inside the legal pages.
  • No API route either: globalyai-website/src/app contains no route.ts, so the marketing site currently has no server surface to post a lead to.

2.2 Lead conversion — notification to Globaly

Not built

Globaly receives an email notification when a lead is generated.

  • Follows from the above: no submission path, nothing to notify on. Cal.com's own booking email is the only alert today.
  • backend/src/shared/mail/templates.ts has OTP, claim, enquiry (unlocked / lead / claim), guide delivery, chat summary and waitlist. No inbound-lead template.
Smallest path One route handler on the marketing app that posts to the existing backend, reusing the waitlist module's store plus a new template — rather than a second lead table. Decide whether website leads should land in waitlist, in enquiries, or in a CRM.
3

Institution invitation & registration

Partial

Institutions should not have direct access to the standard public signup flow. Instead, a Super Admin generates and sends a unique invitation link to each institution after the sales/verification process.

3.1 Invitation contents

Partial

Institution/business name · recipient name · recipient email · unique token/link · invitation status · invitation history. Each invitation individually tracked.

  • Built: a 32-byte token with a 72-hour TTL, minted per institution and emailed as ${WEB_APP_URL}/invite/institution/accept?token=… — sendInstitutionClaimRequest, superadmin/platform/businesses/services/businesses.service.ts:469 (business twin at :418). Name and recipient come off the row; the recipient is the owner's address when there is an owner, otherwise the contact email extraction captured, and that choice deliberately mirrors what acceptInstitutionClaim resolves against.
  • Not built: there is no invitation record. claim_token and claim_token_expires_at are two columns on the institution/business row, so a resend overwrites the previous token in place. No history, no per-invitation status, nothing to list.
Gap — “each invitation should be individually tracked” needs a table Today's shape cannot express it. The precedent already exists in the codebase: both member_invitations (institution staff) and agent_invitations (business staff) are per-invitation rows with status, invited_by, expired_at. The institution claim should become a third table of the same shape rather than a third pair of columns.

3.1 Separate template when data already exists

Not built

If the institution already has existing data in GlobalyApp, a separate invitation email/template should be used.

  • One template serves every case — claimBusinessEmail (shared/mail/templates.ts:751) is sent for businesses, for institutions, and whether or not the row carries extracted data. The institution send even carries a standing note that institution-specific copy was deferred (businesses.service.ts:502).

3.2 Invitation management area

Partial

Super Admin area to create, send, view status, track usage, resend, revoke, and handle expired invitations.

  • Create — Done. Admin adds the institution/business (app/admin/platform/businesses/components/add-business-view.tsx), or it arrives by promotion from an extraction job.
  • Send — Done, singly and in bulk. claim-request-dialog.tsx → sendClaimRequest; bulk goes through queueBulkClaimRequests → the job:business-claim-requests worker, with an inline fallback when the queue is down.
  • View status — Partial. A single badge driven by the row's status / claim_status (unverified → claim_pending → claimed), filterable in the admin list. There is no invitation list and no “sent at / opened / used at”.
  • Resend — Partial. Pressing send again mints a new token over the old one. It works, but it is indistinguishable from a first send and silently invalidates the link already in the recipient's inbox.
  • Revoke — Not built. No endpoint, no UI; revoke appears nowhere in the claim code.
  • Expired — Not built as an admin action. Expiry is only read at accept time; nothing surfaces or sweeps expired invitations.

3.2 Hide the public signup page

Not built

The existing public signup page should be hidden/disabled for the MVP.

  • /signup is live and linked (frontend/src/app/signup/, plus /auth/sign-up). POST /businesses/register backs it (businesses.routes.ts:31) and is not gated on an invitation.
  • Note before disabling it: the signup page is also where the existing-data claim offer appears (§5.1), so hiding the page removes that path too unless it moves.

3.3 Invitation states

Partial

Pending · Sent · Accepted/Used · Expired · Revoked.

  • Expressible today: Sent (claim_pending) and Accepted (claimed). Expired is implicit — a timestamp compared at accept time, never a state. Pending (created, not yet sent) and Revoked do not exist.
  • The guard at businesses.service.ts:424 refuses to re-send to an already-claimed row, which is the one piece of state-machine behaviour present.

3.3 Expired / revoked / used link handling

Not built

Explain the status clearly · offer “Request a New Link” · notify the admin · let the admin resend.

  • Every failure collapses into one message. claim-accept-view.tsx:59 catches any error and renders “Claim link invalid” — expired, already used, revoked and malformed are indistinguishable to the user. The backend does distinguish them (“This claim link has expired”, institution-claim.service.ts:124), so the copy is there; the page just overwrites it on some paths and offers no next action.
  • No “Request a New Link” button, and no admin notification on a failed claim attempt.
Half of this already exists elsewhere requestClaimByEmail (modules/businesses/services/businesses.service.ts:368) is exactly the self-serve “send me a new link” action, wired today only into the signup claim-offer card. Pointing the expired-link page at it is a small change; the admin notification is the new part.

Institution registration & onboarding

Partial

4.1 Registration from a valid invitation link

Partial

Valid link → registration → single registration form (not the two-step flow) → email/OTP verification → redirect to the Business Portal after login.

  • Built: the accept page collects first/last name and calls acceptInstitutionClaim, which creates the claimant's platform_user from those names, writes them onto the listing, and hands off to sign-in (frontend/src/app/invite/claim-accept-view.tsx).
  • Built: OTP verification (otpEmail, templates.ts:683) promotes account_status 0 → 1 and issues the session, so the user lands logged in.
  • Not done — single form. Business/institution onboarding is still two steps: TOTAL_STEPS = 2 in frontend/src/app/business/onboarding-view.tsx:29 (details step, then profile step).
Open — which form does an invited institution see? An invited claimant fills the name form and then signs in; the two-step onboarding is the self-signup path. If the requirement is that both converge on one single-page form, say so — it changes whether onboarding-view.tsx is collapsed or simply bypassed for invited users.

4.2 First-time user experience

Not built

Welcome screens, short explanations, simple animations, key feature introductions — before the dashboard.

  • Nothing of the sort exists. A search across the portal for welcome / tour / walkthrough / first-run components returns only the messages empty-state. New users land directly on the dashboard.

4.3 Onboarding checklist

Done

The Home page should contain an onboarding checklist showing the actions required to complete setup.

  • businesses/services/onboarding-progress.service.ts builds the list and serves it at GET /businesses/me/onboarding; the portal renders it in app/business/portal/components/get-set-up-checklist.tsx.
  • Six steps: create account · verify email · extract your website data (institution categories only) · review courses & services · customise your AI assistant · add the chat widget · invite your team.
  • Each step's done is computed from real state, not from a stored cursor — the widget step flips when a config exists, “installed” requires an actual visitor row, “invite team” requires a second member or a pending invitation.
Open question — order Can onboarding steps be completed in any order, or must they be sequential? As built they are order-independent: every step evaluates itself, nothing is locked. Sequential would be the new work, not the status quo.
Open question — two sequences The requirement asks for a separate sequence for institutions with existing data and institutions without. Not built: the one conditional in the list is isInstitutionCategory, which hides the extraction step for non-institution categories. Nothing branches on whether data already exists.
5

Existing institution data

Partial

If GlobalyApp already has data for an institution, the user should reach the platform with that data already available. The Home page should show: overview of existing data · current data/extraction status · relevant onboarding actions · AI Counsellor setup status · option to obtain/copy the AI widget script.

5.0 Home page content

Partial
  • Data/extraction status — Done. GET /businesses/me/extraction-status → getSelfServiceStatus (data-extraction/services/jobs.service.ts:141) returns { status, progress_pct, counts } over courses, fees, intakes, eligibility, scholarships, study units, study options, accreditations, branches, agents and visa services. Rendered by extraction-progress-card.tsx and site-urls-card.tsx.
  • Onboarding actions — Done (the §4.3 checklist).
  • AI Counsellor setup status — Partial. The checklist's customize_assistant / add_chat_widget steps carry it; there is no counsellor status panel on Home.
  • Widget script — Partial. The snippet lives on /business/ai-widget, not on Home; Home only links there.
  • Overview of existing data — Partial. The counts above are the only overview, and they come from source_job_id — an institution whose data arrived by any route other than a linked extraction job shows zeroes.

5.1 Existing data detection

Partial

Institution data may not be associated with the same email used during lead capture or registration, so the system should not rely solely on email matching. A warning/confirmation mechanism should be presented… [requirement text ends here]

  • Built, and built exactly the way the requirement rules out: detection is email-only. findUnclaimedBusinessByContactEmail matches the address typed at signup against the listing's own contact email; a hit renders the claim-offer card (frontend/src/app/signup/components/business-claim-offer-card.tsx) — “Yes, claim it” / “No, use another email” — and requestClaimByEmail mails the claim link.
  • That card is also the only warning/confirmation mechanism in place, and it resolves silently on a miss by design (anti-enumeration: no found/not-found signal).
  • Not built: matching on institution name, website domain, or any fuzzy candidate set; a disambiguation step when several rows match; and any admin-side review of a claimed match.
Decision needed before this can be built Non-email matching and anti-enumeration pull against each other — showing “we think you are X University” to an unauthenticated visitor leaks that X is in the database. Likely answer: match on domain of the work email plus admin confirmation, rather than open name search. Confirm the intended match keys and who approves a match, then this becomes a small service change.

Appendix — areas awaiting requirement text

The overview lists five further areas whose detailed requirements have not been supplied. Status below is read from code only, so nothing from the previous status document is lost while the requirements are written.

A · Institution data extraction & management

Partial
  • Pipeline — Done. site_map → site_snapshot → site_analysis → url_classify → queue_pages (data-extraction/schemas/step.schema.ts:27), driven by lib/pipeline-steps.ts and the extraction-job / page / step / verify / queue-reclaim / schedule workers. Pages become one Markdown snapshot each (lib/page-store.ts); writes go through lib/staging-writer.ts; completion is reconciled in lib/queue-completion.ts.
  • Self-service start — Partial. POST /businesses/me/start-extraction takes a row lock and refuses a second job once source_job_id is set (businesses.service.ts:233), but throws “Extraction is only available for institutions” unless the category passes isInstitutionCategory (:236), and the card hides itself for everyone else (start-extraction-card.tsx:41).
  • URL review — Partial. getExtractionSiteUrls forces excluded: false: the institution can read the list, edit a page's Markdown and request a re-fetch of up to 10 URLs, but cannot deselect pages before the crawl. Exclusion stays an admin action by design — confirm whether self-service should expose it.
  • Completion email — Not built. lib/queue-completion.ts:116 writes an extraction_complete job event and that string appears nowhere else in the codebase: no consumer, no template. Needs an extractionCompleteEmail, a recipient lookup from source_job_id, and a re-send guard — Resume and Re-run both re-enter completion.

B · Business & contact management

Partial
  • Profile CRUD at GET|PATCH /businesses/me, AI-drafted profile copy via POST /me/ai-assist, review confirmation via POST /me/onboarding/review-courses.
  • Staff invitations exist per tenant and are per-invitation rows — member_invitations (institutions) and agent_invitations (businesses), with resend supported from the admin side (resendInstitutionInvitation).
  • Widget contacts: POST /ai-chat/guest/contact records a visitor's name and email on the tenant visitor row (visitor.service.ts), surfaced in app/business/ai-widget/visitors/ with a detail drawer and edit dialogs.
  • Gap: no email to the business when a lead hands over contact details — the only mail on that path is chatSummaryEmail, which goes to the visitor.

C · AI counsellor & knowledge management

Partial
  • Retrieval over the institution's own extracted data and crawled site, with a per-topic money guard (fees / refund / scholarship / living) in services/rag.service.ts.
  • Institution memory is complete on the backend: GET|POST /institution/memories, :id/approve, :id/deprecate, :id/reactivate, :id/unflag, DELETE, plus GET /institution/conversations and /:id/messages.
  • Gap — no portal UI. No frontend file references institution/memories or institution/conversations. Today an institution cannot see, approve or deprecate what its counsellor has learned.

Done
