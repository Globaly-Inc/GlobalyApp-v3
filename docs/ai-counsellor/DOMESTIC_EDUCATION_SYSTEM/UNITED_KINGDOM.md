# United Kingdom — Domestic Education Systems

**Document purpose:** Reference knowledge so the GlobalyApp AI counsellor can interpret what a UK student's stated education *means inside the UK*, and — because the UK is a major GlobalyApp destination — so the AI understands the receiving systems' own structures precisely.
**Research date:** 2026-08-21
**Scope:** Domestic systems only.

> ## ⚠ THE UK IS FOUR EDUCATION SYSTEMS, NOT ONE
>
> UK ENIC's own guide states it plainly: *"The devolved administrations of the UK govern their own primary and secondary education systems. While the system and awards offered in **England, Northern Ireland, and Wales are broadly similar**, the **Scottish system is quite distinct** from its counterparts."* ✅
>
> This document is structured accordingly: shared/UK-wide material first, then **England**, **Northern Ireland**, **Wales** and **Scotland** separately.

> ## ⚠ THE SEVEN THINGS THAT MOST OFTEN GO WRONG WITH THE UK
>
> 1. **GCSE grading differs by nation**: **9–1 in England**, **A*–G in Wales and Northern Ireland**, with Northern Ireland adding a **C\* grade in 2019**. ✅
> 2. **Northern Ireland's year numbering is offset by one from England's.** NI Years 11–12 = England Years 10–11 (both Key Stage 4). ✅
> 3. **At the four Scottish "ancient" universities (Aberdeen, Edinburgh, Glasgow, St Andrews), the undergraduate degree is titled "MA" or "MA (Hons)"** — a **bachelor-level** degree named "Master of Arts". ✅
> 4. **UK medicine, dentistry and veterinary first degrees (MB BS, MB ChB, BDS, BVSc, BVMS) sit at FHEQ level 7 / SCQF level 11 — master's level.** ✅
> 5. **A Scottish Honours degree is 4 years / 480 SCQF credits**; an England/Wales/NI Honours degree is **3 or 4 years / 360–480 CATS points**. ✅
> 6. **"Bachelor (Ordinary)" is Pass/Fail** and is a different qualification from Bachelor with Honours (which is classified 1st / 2.1 / 2.2 / 3rd). ✅
> 7. **A UK credit is 10 notional learning hours** (CATS and SCQF alike) — **not** the same unit as an ECTS credit (25–30 hours), an Indian credit (30 hours) or a Bangladeshi credit (40 hours). ✅

# 2. UK Qualification Frameworks

**The UK has four current frameworks plus a Scottish higher-education sub-framework.** ✅

| Framework | Full name | Covers | Levels | Jurisdiction |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-------:|--------------|
| **RQF** | Regulated Qualifications Framework | *"Non-higher education qualifications"* — general and vocational | **8 levels + 3 entry levels** | **England and Northern Ireland** ✅ |
| **FHEQ** | Framework for Higher Education Qualifications | Qualifications awarded by degree-awarding bodies | **Levels 4–8** | **England, Northern Ireland and Wales** ✅ |
| **SCQF** | Scottish Credit and Qualifications Framework | *"all Scottish mainstream qualifications"* | **12 levels** | **Scotland** ✅ |
| **CQFW** | Credit and Qualifications Framework for Wales | *"higher education, regulated vocational and general"* education and training | Same nine levels as the RQF ⚠ | **Wales** ✅ |
| **FQHEIS** | Framework for Qualifications of Higher Education Institutions in Scotland | Scottish higher education | **Corresponds to SCQF levels 7–12** | Scotland ✅ |

**Superseded frameworks** ✅ — relevant for older UK qualifications:

| Framework | Period |
|-----------|--------|

| **NQF** — National Qualifications Framework | **to 2008** |

> ✅⚠ **Note the single most useful line in this table:** a **GCSE at grades 9–4 (A\*–C) is RQF level 2**, while a **GCSE at grades 3–1 (D–G) is RQF level 1**. **The same qualification sits at a different framework level depending on the grade achieved.** This is unusual and important: in the UK, the *grade* determines the *level* for GCSEs. ✅

## 2.5 The FHEQ ↔ FQHEIS ↔ SCQF ↔ QF-EHEA mapping ✅

Reproduced from UK ENIC's guide:

| FHEQ level | SCQF/FQHEIS level | Typical higher education qualifications | QF-EHEA (Bologna) |
|-----------:|------------------:|------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **8** | **12** | **Doctoral degrees** (e.g. **PhD / DPhil, EdD, DBA**) | **Third cycle** (end-of-cycle) qualifications |
| **7** | **11** | **Master's degrees** (e.g. MA, MSc, MPhil, MRes); **Integrated Master's degrees** (e.g. MChem, MEng, MPhys); **primary qualifications (or first degrees) in medicine, dentistry and veterinary science** (e.g. **MB BS, MB ChB, BDS, BVSc, BVMS**); **Postgraduate Diplomas**; **Postgraduate Certificates** | **Second cycle** (end-of-cycle) qualifications |
| **6** | **10** | **Bachelor degrees with Honours** (e.g. BA Hons, BSc Hons) | **First cycle** (end-of-cycle) qualifications |
| — | **9** | **Bachelor degrees** (e.g. BA, BSc) — *ordinary/pass degrees* | — |

> ✅⚠ **THE MEDICINE/DENTISTRY/VETERINARY FACT.** UK **MB BS, MB ChB, BDS, BVSc and BVMS** are *"primary qualifications (or first degrees)"* placed at **FHEQ level 7 / SCQF level 11 — master's level**, aligned to the **Bologna second cycle**. ✅
>
> ⚠ **Contrast with Canada**, whose own framework says MD/DDS/JD are *"considered to be bachelor's programs in academic standing"*. **The same profession, opposite framework placements, in two English-speaking systems.** The AI must never generalise from one to the other.

# 3. Credit Systems — UK-wide ✅

| System | Definition | Where |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **CATS** — Credit Accumulation and Transfer Scheme | *"one credit (or credit point)"* = **10 hours of notional learning time** — *"the time, on average, a learner takes to achieve the specified learning outcomes"* | *"the national … scheme … used by many higher education institutions"* across the UK ✅ |
| **SCQF credit points** | **1 SCQF credit point = 10 notional learning hours** — *"the time spent on all activities that contribute to the achievement of the learning outcomes … includes those that take place before and after delivery as well as the actual delivery itself"* | Scotland ✅ |
| **ECTS** | **1 ECTS credit = 25–30 hours of student workload** | *"widely referred to by UK higher education institutions in transcripts where the European Diploma Supplement or Higher Education Achievement Report (HEAR) is produced"* ✅ |
| **TQT** — Total Qualification Time | *"encompasses both taught hours and self-study"* | RQF (England, NI) ✅ |

> ✅⚠ **The credit-comparison table the AI should hold in mind:**
>
> | System | Hours per credit |
> |--------|-----------------:|
> | **UK CATS / SCQF** | **10** ✅ |
> | **ECTS** | **25–30** ✅ |
> | **India (NCrF)** | **30** ✅ |
> | **Bangladesh (BNQF)** | **40** (lecture); 60 (lab); 80 (workplace) ✅ |
>
> **These are four different units.** A UK 360-credit bachelor's honours degree and an Indian 120-credit bachelor's degree both represent roughly 3,600 notional hours — the *credit counts differ by a factor of three because the units differ*. **The AI must never compare raw credit numbers across countries**, and should reason in **notional hours** or **years of full-time study** when a comparison is unavoidable. ✅

# 4. Higher Education Institutions — UK-wide ✅

| Category | Meaning | Verification |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| **Recognised Body** | An institution with **degree-awarding powers** — it awards its own degrees | ✅ |
| **Listed Body** | An institution that **delivers courses leading to another body's degrees** but does not award its own | ✅ |
| **Degree-awarding powers** | The formal authority to award degrees | ✅ |
| **University Title (UT)** | The right to call itself a university | ✅ |

> ✅ **The recognition test for a UK qualification:** was it **awarded by a Recognised Body**? UK ENIC's guide has a section titled *"Where can I find a list of recognised higher education institutions?"* ✅ — i.e. an authoritative list exists. ❌ *The list's location was not captured; obtain it from UK ENIC / GOV.UK.*

> ⚠ **A real counselling implication:** a student may have **studied at a Listed Body** while their **degree was awarded by a different (Recognised) institution**. The AI must ask **who awarded the degree**, not only where the student studied — the same rule as Nepal's and India's affiliated-college models. ✅

> ⚠⚠ **NORTHERN IRELAND'S YEAR NUMBERING IS OFFSET BY ONE FROM ENGLAND'S.** ✅
> - **NI Year 12 = the end of Key Stage 4 = the GCSE year** — the equivalent of **England's Year 11**.
> - **NI Year 14** is the final school year — the equivalent of **England's Year 13**.
>
> **A Northern Irish student saying "I finished Year 12" has finished GCSEs, not A levels.** An English student saying "I finished Year 12" has completed the first year of A levels. **This is one of the highest-risk misreadings in the entire UK system**, and it is invisible unless the AI knows to ask which UK nation.

# 29. UK-Specific Complexity Summary

The seven things that most often go wrong when interpreting a UK student's education:

1. **GCSE grading differs by nation** — 9–1 (England), A\*–G (Wales/NI), plus NI's **C\*** from 2019. (§7, §11, §14)
2. **Northern Ireland's year numbering is offset by one** — NI Year 12 = England Year 11. (§10)
3. **"MA (Hons)" at Aberdeen, Edinburgh, Glasgow and St Andrews is an undergraduate degree.** (§19)
4. **UK medicine/dentistry/veterinary first degrees are FHEQ 7 / SCQF 11** — master's level, unlike Canada's. (§2.5)
5. **A Bachelor (Ordinary)/Pass degree is Pass/Fail with no classification** — a different qualification from Bachelor with Honours. (§21)
6. **"Graduate" (FHEQ 6) and "Postgraduate" (FHEQ 7) are different levels.** (§22)
7. **A UK credit is 10 notional hours** — not an ECTS credit (25–30), an Indian credit (30) or a Bangladeshi credit (40). (§3)

Plus: **Scotland's Honours degree is 4 years / 480 credits** by design (§20); **GCSE grade determines RQF level** (9–4 = level 2; 3–1 = level 1) (§2.4); **"college" is ambiguous** across four different meanings (§25); and students in GlobalyApp's core markets usually hold **International A levels / IGCSEs**, not domestic UK qualifications (§24).
