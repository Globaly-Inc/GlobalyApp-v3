# Official Source Directory

**Research date:** 2026-08-21
**Distinct URLs captured across the eight country documents:** 243
**Purpose:** the consolidated register of authorities and sources for the domestic education systems of the eight research countries.

## Authority tiers used

| Tier | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Tier 1** | Government / official — ministries, national education authorities, government qualification frameworks, national examination boards, curriculum authorities, accreditation bodies, official statistical agencies, government education portals, **and national information centres for qualification recognition** |
| **Tier 2** | Official education institutions — universities, colleges, schools, examination boards, accreditation organisations, professional education bodies |
| **Tier 3** | International organisations and recognised credential-evaluation bodies — UNESCO, OECD, World Bank, UNICEF, ILO, ENIC/NARIC-network bodies, WES/WENR, Nuffic, TAICEP, AACRAO, APNNIC, CHEA, British Council |
| **Tier 4** | Academic research — peer-reviewed journals, university research, comparative education studies |
| **Secondary** | Used **only for corroboration**, never as a primary basis. Listed per country at the end of each block. |
