Omnichannel AI Inbox

AI Embed, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook in one inbox. Connect your channels. Let AI handle the conversations. Step in whenever you need to. Everything here runs on mock data: nothing talks to Zernio, Meta or the AI backend.

Tip: start with a guided journey above, or click around freely. “Act as the customer” sends messages into whichever conversation is open.

This is the build plan behind the prototype. It explains how the Inbox at /business/messages grows from one channel (AI Embed) into four: AI Embed, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger. It is written so a developer can start from it and a reviewer can check decisions before code exists. Anything not yet confirmed is marked Assumption or listed under Open questions.

Summary

Customers write to the business on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger or the website widget. Every message lands in one Inbox. AI answers first, on every channel, using the same knowledge and profile context AI Embed uses today. When AI can't or shouldn't answer, the conversation is flagged as Needs attention and moves to the top of the Inbox. A team member takes over, AI pauses, and the team member replies from the Inbox; the reply goes out on the customer's own channel. When they're done they click Resume AI and AI carries on.

What changes

  • The Inbox lists conversations from all four channels, with filters by status and by channel.
  • The existing Channels page gets working Connect buttons for WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.
  • Takeover gets a confirmation and an explicit Resume AI button. As today, AI also takes back over after 15 minutes with no team reply.
  • Conversations can be starred and summarised by AI.
  • One customer can have several channels; their profile (Customer 360) shows all of them.
  • Contacts → Visitors & Leads lists everyone from every channel, one row per person, with a channel filter.

What stays the same

  • AI Embed keeps its tables, widget, settings page and API. It is read into the new Inbox, not migrated.
  • The look of the Inbox: same shell, rows, bubbles, Aly orb, notes and composer.
  • The AI reply path (RAG + profile context) is reused for every channel.
  • Nothing goes live per organisation until a feature flag is switched on.
  • No assignment: whoever takes over handles the conversation, as today.
  • Notifications are out of scope for now. The team finds work through the Needs Attention filter, and AI Embed's existing handover email is unchanged.

Principles

  1. AI is the default responder. A new conversation is always mode = ai. The team should never have to watch every message.
  2. The team handles the exceptions. The Inbox puts Needs attention first, with its own filter and count. Everything else can stay with AI.
  3. One answerer at a time. Either AI or a person replies, never both. The server checks mode before any AI reply is sent, so a race between a takeover and an AI reply can't produce a double answer.
  4. Nothing internal leaks. Internal notes, AI summaries and suggestions are stored apart from the transcript the customer and the model read.
  5. One customer, many channels, one profile. Channels link to the same contact only on exact matches (same phone or email). Never by name.
  6. Build on what exists. Existing pages are extended in place: the Channels page, the inbox bar, the widget settings page.

How a message flows

Inbound: a customer writes on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook

  1. Zernio calls our webhook. One endpoint for every organisation: POST /webhooks/zernio. It checks the X-Zernio-Signature header (hex HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body, keyed by our webhook secret), skips any X-Zernio-Event-Id it has already stored (Zernio sends the same id on every retry and redelivery), stores the raw event and answers 200 within a second. It does no other work, so Zernio never times out and retries.
  2. A worker picks the event up from the queue (same pattern as the existing workers) and finds the organisation: every event carries the Zernio account.id (or profileId for account events), and the shared zernio_accounts lookup says which business or institution owns it. Only then does it open that tenant's database. An unknown or disconnected account is logged and dropped.
  3. Find or create the contact. Look up contact_identities by (channel, external id). If none exists, create a contact and the identity. If the payload carries a phone or email that exactly matches another contact, link to it instead (see Channel rules).
  4. Find or create the conversation. One open conversation per contact per channel. A message to a resolved conversation reopens it with mode = ai.
  5. Store the message with its provider_message_id. A duplicate id is ignored, which makes retries safe. Update last_inbound_at (it drives the reply window) and bump unread for every team member.
  6. Decide who answers. If mode = human, stop: the team member sees it in the Inbox. If the status is “customer requested human”, stop. Otherwise run AI.
  7. AI answers through the existing reply path, with the contact's facts from every channel as context. Before sending, re-read mode; if someone took over meanwhile, drop the reply.
  8. Send through Zernio with POST /v1/inbox/conversations/{conversationId}/messages (body: accountId, text, optional attachments or template) and an Idempotency-Key header, so a retried send is never delivered twice. While AI is writing, GlobalyApp calls POST …/typing so the customer sees it typing. Sends to one person are paced (WhatsApp rejects bursts of more than about 10 a minute with error 131056). Store the AI message as queued, then sent, delivered, read or failed from Zernio's message.* events.
  9. Escalation checks run alongside the reply. If one fires, the conversation becomes Needs attention and moves to the top of the Inbox.

Outbound: a team member replies

  1. The composer is only enabled when mode = human and the current user is the handler.
  2. POST /inbox/conversations/:id/messages checks the same on the server, then checks the channel's reply window. A free-form message outside the window is refused with a clear error; the UI offers templates instead.
  3. The message is stored as queued and sent through Zernio (or written straight to the transcript for AI Embed, as today).
  4. A failed send shows a red “Not delivered · Retry” under the bubble.

UI walkthrough

Channels page (existing, extended)

“Connect a channel” in the inbox bar already links to /business/messages/channels. The page keeps its layout: Back to Inbox, the Web widget card, then Available channels. Each channel card has three looks:

  • Not connected: dashed border, description, and a Connect button where “Coming soon” is today.
  • Connected: solid border, the account and number or handle, WhatsApp's quality rating (with an amber warning when Meta rates it red), whether AI is answering, and links for AI configuration, Manage and Disconnect.

A line above the cards says the channels run through Zernio and whether Zernio is operational.

AI behaviour

Answering

Every channel uses the AI Embed reply path: retrieval over the business's knowledge plus profile context. The difference is the context source. Today lib/visitor-context.ts maps the visitor row into the prompt; it will read the contact's facts instead, which include what the customer said on other channels. Channel formatting differs: WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger get plain text with short paragraphs and no markdown tables or cards.

Escalation detectors

ConditionHow it's detectedWhat AI says
Customer asks for a humanThe existing judgeWantsHuman in lib/handover-detect.ts: a regex prefilter, then a separate model call before the reply.“Absolutely. I'll connect you with our team…” then silence.
AI is unsureLow retrieval confidence, or topics on a deny list (visa chances, admission guarantees).“This question may require assistance from our admissions team…”
Sensitive questionJudge call for refusals, complaints, legal or personal hardship.A short holding reply, then no further AI answers on that topic.

Detectors are separate calls, not instructions inside the reply prompt. If the model provider falls back to a small model, in-reply instructions get ignored, so escalation can't rely on them.

Summary and suggestions

  • Summary card: built from the existing staff summary (STAFF_SUMMARY_SYSTEM and parseStaffSummary in lib/conversation-summary.ts), extended with “current question” and “recommended next step”. Generated on takeover if the stored one is stale.
  • Suggestions: one model call returns a short hint and a ready-to-send draft. Generate reply and Improve response are separate on-demand calls. All are staff-only and never written to the transcript.
  • Suggest-only mode: AI writes its reply as a draft on the conversation instead of sending it; the conversation becomes “AI draft to review”; taking over puts the draft in the composer.

Backend plan

  • New module backend/src/modules/inbox/: conversations, messages, notes, stars and reads. Routes, services and repositories in the existing module shape.
  • New module backend/src/modules/channels/: connections, AI configuration, templates, the webhook route and the Zernio adapter.
  • Zernio adapter, the only file that knows Zernio: createProfile, connectUrl, listOptions, select, health, sendMessage (text, attachments, template, idempotency key), sendTyping, takeThreadControl, listTemplates, listConversations (backfill), verifyWebhook, parseWebhook. A fake implementation backs local development and tests.
  • Webhook route resolves the organisation through zernio_accounts before touching any tenant data, the same rule as other public routes.
  • Workers:
    • channel:inbound processes stored events in order per conversation.
    • channel:send sends with retries, backoff and per-recipient pacing.
    • channel:backfill pages GET /v1/inbox/conversations?profileId=… every few minutes to pick up anything whose webhook never arrived.
    • channel:health runs a daily GET /v1/accounts/health as a safety net. Day to day, health comes from account.disconnected and the WhatsApp status events, so no polling.
  • AI Embed adapter: reads ai_widget_visitors and its sessions into the conversation shape and writes mode and status back through takeover.service.ts. AI Embed never goes through Zernio.
  • Takeover generalised: the mode/status rules become one pure function, like whoAnswers today, so they can be unit-tested without a database.
  • Reference: Zernio docs (docs.zernio.com: multi-tenant, multi-tenant/inbox) and the OpenAPI spec (zernio.com/openapi.yaml).

Testing and verification

  • Pure unit tests (no database, like tests/ai-takeover.ts): the mode/status transitions, the reply-window rule, contact matching (exact phone or email only), and webhook parsing for each channel.
  • Database tests, run locally by a developer: duplicate webhook events store one message; a takeover between AI reply generation and sending drops the AI reply; two people taking over at once leaves one handler.
  • Frontend: tsc and eslint, then the guided journeys clicked through in a browser.
  • Pilot: one internal organisation with a test WhatsApp number, Instagram account and Facebook Page before any customer organisation.

Open questions

Each one has a recommendation; none blocks phase 1.

1. Channel status chips in the inbox bar?Recommendation: keep them; they are small and make a lost connection visible from the Inbox.
2. Suggest-only mode in v1?Recommendation: keep it; it's what cautious businesses will pick first.
3. Stars per person or shared?Recommendation: per person, like unread.
4. Who pays for Zernio?Zernio is free up to 2 connected accounts and usage-based after that, and its inbox endpoints need the Inbox add-on on our Zernio team. An organisation that connects WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook uses 3 accounts. Recommendation: decide before launch whether the cost is absorbed or passed on, for example through chat credits.
5. Rename “Visitors” to “Contacts”?The page now holds everyone from every channel, not only widget visitors. Recommendation: keep “Visitors & Leads” for now (no change for existing users) and revisit once WhatsApp is live.
6. Which WhatsApp numbers can be connected?Zernio can connect a number the business already uses (through Meta's signup, optionally keeping the WhatsApp Business phone app) or provide a new number with its own verification steps. Recommendation: v1 connects existing numbers only, with coexistence allowed.
7. Use WhatsApp quick-reply buttons later?Zernio's send call supports buttons and quick replies on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, so AI could offer tappable choices like the widget's cards. Recommendation: not in v1; plain text first.
