International students

Over half of our students come from outside Australia. The international team handles the paperwork so you can get on with choosing a course.

  • Merit and regional scholarships up to 30% of tuition
  • Conditional offers within five working days
  • Airport pickup, accommodation help and orientation week
  • Post-study work rights advice from a registered migration agent
  • English pathways on campus — no separate provider to enrol with
